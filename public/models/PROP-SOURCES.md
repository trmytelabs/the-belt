# Vehicle and devil source assets

Retrieved 2026-10-18. These assets are individually listed by their creator,
Quaternius, as **Public Domain (CC0)** on Poly Pizza. The source GLBs are
unchanged; scale, paint, glass, lighting and the devil's purple tint are applied
at runtime. The devil's trident is hidden in the game.

Creator: [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/). The author's
[Ultimate Monsters page](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html)
also identifies its animated models as CC0.

The cars retain the existing game colours, headlights and driving roots.
The same devil model is used on Ben's shoulder and in the portal scene.
The full-size devil retains the existing emerge, drag and sink staging, with the model's
idle, reaching and walking animation clips. The procedural art remains as a
fallback if a model cannot load. Both upgrades are used only by the 3D game.
